Navigation, v4: phone, tablet and laptop

Changes: Contests gets a tab with a LIVE badge, the selected tab is a solid ink pill, and the bar floats in notebook style (ink outline, hard shadow, bolder icons). It's centred and responsive: it fills a phone, caps at 440px wide and centres on tablets. The sidebar takes over from 1024px up. Each variant is shown at phone width (360px) and tablet width, in light and dark.

A · Home · Roadmap · Contests · AI Chat · More

Career Matches moves under More. It's still one tap from Home, because the dashboard leads with your top matches. Roadmap stays in the bar as the "what do I do next" screen.

B · Home · Matches · Contests · AI Chat · More

Roadmap moves under More, but it's still reachable from each career card and from the dashboard's "next step" card. Shown with Contests selected so you can see the active pill and LIVE badge together.

Laptop / desktop (1024px and up): the sidebar

The sidebar lists every section, because desktop has room. It uses the same ink pill for the selected item and the same LIVE badge as the phone bar. It's grouped into My path (Home, Matches, Roadmap), Discover (Explore, Career Space) and Do (Contests, AI Chat). Your profile card at the bottom opens Settings and has the light/dark toggle. Content is capped at about 1200px and centred on very wide screens.
